The story engine

A classified page. A buried memory. A woman trained to distrust herself.

Page 41 is an original NCCA project in development. After a brilliant security architect survives a staged ambush that kills the man she loves, her trusted mentor uses trauma, medicine, and institutional power to rewrite her memory and turn her into the public face of his covert empire.

“They can alter the record. They cannot silence what the body remembers.”

Passport structure

The elements that hold the title together.

01 / Engine

Memory against the machine

Each recovered memory threatens the covert architecture built around Kat and exposes another person who helped erase her.

02 / Tone

Statecraft and paranoia

Elegant diplomatic power collides with intimate psychological suspense.

03 / Lead

Kat Sullivan

A security architect fighting to prove the official story is a lie while reclaiming her own history.

04 / Visual Language

Classified paper / clinical control

Redactions, pressure marks, page numbers, and institutional spaces become part of the mystery.

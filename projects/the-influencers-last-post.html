The story engine

Her perfect image was shattered by murder.

The Influencer’s Last Post is an original NCCA project in development. When a social media influencer’s final post becomes the key to solving her own murder, the story behind the screen becomes the only way to separate performance from evidence.

“The algorithm knows who did it before the detective does.”

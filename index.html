About the Filmmaker

Kelly Thomas is an Atlanta-based writer and filmmaker with 186 episodes of television under her belt, including long-term roles on One Tree Hill. Her work blends Southern Gothic storytelling with genre-bending thrillers, socially conscious dramas, and bold comedies that center Black voices, female agency, and cultural truth.

With 52 original projects in development — from limited series to feature films — Kelly is building a studio that doesn’t just entertain, but transforms.

ABOUT THE FILMMAKER

I'm a writer and producer who blends the storytelling heart of the South with a modern, gritty edge. From gritty crime dramas set in Atlanta's underworld to heartwarming holiday comedies featuring viral TikToks and dollar-store wreaths, I navigate genres like a showrunner in sneakers.

I craft worlds where FBI agents clash with cartel bosses, artists lead double lives, and Grandma might just become the next social media sensation. With a producer's mind, a writer's wit, and a director's eye for both tension and tenderness, my scripts are not just narratives but living, breathing embodiments of culture, conflict, and comedy.

Over the course of my career, I've contributed to over 30 projects, including 186 episodes of "One Tree Hill." These experiences, from "The Line" to "The Twelve Dates of Christmas Tree Lot," have been instrumental in developing my skills in leadership, creativity, and problem-solving.

MY DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

Every story I tell follows a bold, intentional path — from spark to screen.

Development

Laying the foundation

  • Concept Creation
    Writers brainstorm and develop the core idea that drives the story.
  • Scriptwriting
    The screenplay is drafted, refined, and polished for production-readiness.
  • Financing
    Producers secure funding through investors, studio partners, or grants.

Pre-Production

Prepping for the big picture

  • Planning
    A detailed schedule and production budget are mapped out.
  • Casting
    Actors are auditioned and selected to embody key roles.
  • Location Scouting
    Filming environments are identified, scouted, and secured.
  • Crew Assembly
    Key team members — directors, cinematographers, designers — are hired and briefed.

Production

Bringing the vision to life

  • Filming
    Cameras roll on set, on location, or in studio.
  • Daily Reviews
    Footage is reviewed each day to ensure quality, continuity, and performance.
  • Adjustments
    Creative and logistical tweaks are made in real time as the story unfolds.

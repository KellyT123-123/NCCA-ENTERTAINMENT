ABOUT THE FILMMAKER

Kelly T. crafts character driven stories where Southern soul meets modern edge. His work blends the emotional resonance of Queen Sugar with the narrative boldness of Atlanta, exploring identity, legacy, and justice through a Black Southern lens.. From gritty crime dramas set in Atlanta's underworld to heartwarming holiday comedies featuring viral TikToks and dollar-store wreaths, I navigate genres like a showrunner in sneakers.

He crafts worlds where FBI agents clash with cartel bosses, artists lead double lives, and Grandma might just become the next social media sensation. With a producer's mind, a writer's wit, and a director's eye for both tension and tenderness, his scripts are not just narratives but living, breathing embodiments of culture, conflict, and comedy.

Over the course of his career, he has contributed to over 30 projects, including 186 episodes of "One Tree Hill." These experiences have been instrumental in developing my skills in leadership, creativity, and problem solving.

Catchphrase:
Bring stories to life, capture the imagination, and leave a lasting impression.

About NCCA Entertainment

A boutique content studio delivering premium, culturally rich scripted series and films — where Atlanta's soul meets global storytelling.

Our Mission

NCCA Entertainment specializes in developing and producing market-ready film and television projects that center diverse voices, Southern authenticity, and commercial viability. We create content that resonates across platforms while maintaining cultural integrity and artistic excellence.

What We Do

We develop scripted features, TV movies (MOW), limited series, network series, and docuseries across multiple genres including drama, thriller, romance, comedy, horror, and Southern Gothic. Our projects are designed for leading platforms including BET+, OWN, Lifetime, Netflix, HBO, Peacock, Paramount+, and major broadcast networks.

Our Approach

Atlanta-forward. Culture-first. Franchise-minded. We combine deep cultural knowledge with strategic market positioning to create projects that serve both artistic vision and commercial objectives. Every project is developed with clear target buyers, comprehensive pitch materials, and production readiness.
